Brand rules · Grid system · Continuity guide

Color system
Deep blue
CivikNet
Ice blue
Sand
Ink

Typography rules
  • Headlines: Geist italic, tight tracking -0.02em
  • Display: Geist Bold, -0.04em, 0.9 line-height
  • Body: Geist 400, 18–20px, 1.4 line-height
  • Kickers: Geist Mono, 14px, +0.08em, UPPERCASE only
  • Never: Title Case, emoji, exclamation points
Image asset rule
  • One shared hero gradient across all 8 formats
  • Deep blue → CivikNet blue radial glow, noise overlay
  • Subtle 64px mesh grid lines at 2.5% opacity
  • No photography replacements without matching tone
  • When using photos: cool-toned, abstract, tech infrastructure
  • Apply deep blue color grade at 40–60% overlay
Logo rules
  • White with white border on all dark/gradient surfaces
  • Ink black with ink border on paper/sand surfaces
  • Never tinted, recolored, scaled below minimum size
  • Always top-left corner, 48–80px from edge
  • Minimum clear space: 1× cap-height on all sides
  • The ✦ glyph is always present with the wordmark
Grid · spacing
  • Base unit: 8px
  • Small margins (banners/headers): 80px sides
  • Standard margins (posts): 56–64px all sides
  • Story margins: 80px top/bottom, 64px sides
  • Content safe zone: 90% of canvas dimensions
  • Hairline borders: rgba(255,255,255,0.12) on dark
Voice · copy rules
  • Sentence case for headlines (not Title Case)
  • Specific verbs: Generate. Automate. Govern. Act.
  • Em-dashes for editorial rhythm — like this
  • Numerals stay numerals: 98%, 1,500, 3 hours
  • No first-person singular ("I") in marketing copy
  • Kicker text: UPPERCASE · dot-separated · concise
Platform dimensions
  • Instagram Post: 1080 × 1080 px (1:1)
  • Instagram Story: 1080 × 1920 px (9:16)
  • LinkedIn Post: 1200 × 628 px
  • LinkedIn Banner: 1584 × 396 px
  • Facebook Post: 1200 × 630 px
  • Facebook Cover: 820 × 462 px
  • Twitter/X Post: 1600 × 900 px (16:9)
  • Twitter/X Header: 1500 × 500 px (3:1)
Component rules
  • Pill tags: 999rem radius, hairline border, mono 9–10px
  • CTA buttons: pill shape, CivikNet blue or Warm orange
  • Cards: 1.6rem radius, hairline shadow
  • Hover state: opacity 0.6 only — no color shift or scale
  • Accent bar (banners): 6px CivikNet blue, flush left edge
  • Dividers: 1px hairline, never solid opaque lines
