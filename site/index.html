CivikAccess

CivikAccess is the service coordination platform for frontline public services. It builds the map of how help moves in seconds, and the workers in the room keep it true. It runs without ever sharing who a family is. And families always see what to do next — even when they get stuck.

We route the work, not the family's identity. The platform knows what's happening. It does not know who it's happening to.

Navigation · Reach

Built for the family,
not just the agency.

Most tools are built for workers. They hand a family a list and say “good luck.” That’s why most help fails — no one can reach the family after they walk out the door.

  • A clear path, step by step. Families see what to do next. Then what to do after that.
  • We see when they’re stuck. If a family stops moving forward, we see where. Help reaches them right there.
  • Works anywhere. On a phone. From a printed QR sheet. With or without internet.

The goal is simple: make help frictionless.

Privacy · Trust

Coordination without identification

A family can be supported through an entire pathway — across multiple agencies — without their personal identifying information ever entering CivikAccess in a form that can be surfaced, queried, or extracted. Personal data stays inside the agency that already holds it; the platform routes the work, not the person.

  • The package model. A family moves through the system like a tracked package — followed all the way, without anyone in the middle knowing who’s inside.
  • Names stay at the edge. Personal info lives with the agency, partner, or vendor — the place that already has it. Not in the middle.
  • No central file. There’s no big shared list of everyone in the system. That’s the biggest reason agencies can’t work together — gone.

Access Reliability is the foundation.

AI is already making choices about families — working from broken records no one fully sees. Before AI can be trusted, the picture under it has to be true. CivikAccess builds that picture: every step, every handoff, every person on the hook — so anything built on top of it works the way it should.
