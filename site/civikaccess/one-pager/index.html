CivikAccess
A briefing · State, local, and institutional leadership

Coordination infrastructure for public services. Designed for the intelligence already operating on them.

CivikAccess is a service coordination design platform for frontline public services. It creates a verifiable, human-accountable picture of every family's pathway across the agencies that serve them — without their personal identifying information ever entering the platform.

The problem you're already managing

Public services were built one program at a time. Each program has its own forms, its own intake, its own data system. A family touching five agencies gets described five different ways — a participant here, a patient there, a client elsewhere. Frontline workers patch the gaps with sticky notes and personal phone calls. The coordination work holding the system together is invisible to leadership, and so is where it breaks.

AI is already making decisions in your jurisdiction about who gets help, who gets flagged, who gets prioritized — working from the same fragmented records the case manager already couldn't make sense of.

Why this is an infrastructure problem, not a tool problem

Referral platforms track whether help was offered, not whether it arrived. Case management systems organize records by program, so the same family looks completely different depending on which agency you ask. Consultants map the process and leave a slide deck.

None of them name the pathway as the thing the whole network works from. CivikAccess does — and that's the layer responsible AI in public services can be built on.

Record

Foundation

The verifiable layer responsible AI can be built on.

How it works

01 · Build

Design pathway coordination.

Map the operating model with your network. Define who owns each step. Set what closes a handoff.

02 · Publish

Make the pathway live.

The working pathway becomes visible across the network. See where families are stuck. See where the system is reliable — and where it isn't.

03 · Update

Improve from the field.

Case managers propose changes when reality shifts. Recurring updates become the standard. The workers who proposed them get credit.

Privacy by architecture

A family can be supported through an entire pathway, across multiple agencies, without their personal identifying information ever entering CivikAccess in a form that can be surfaced, queried, or extracted. Personal data stays inside the agency that already holds it. The platform routes the work, not the person.
