Photo Variation System — Layout Rules

Photo Treatment

  • Dark overlay: rgba(9,16,43,0.78) → rgba(0,57,180,0.35)
  • Right-edge fade to match paper divider
  • Grid lines at 3% white opacity over photo
  • CN watermark preserved at 4.5% opacity

Photo Selection

  • Civic, community, or urban subjects only
  • High contrast — no flat or low-light images
  • Portrait or centered subjects work best
  • Source: Unsplash free license

Right Zone

  • Always white (#ffffff) — never gradient
  • 3px CivikNet blue (#0057f3) left divider
  • Headline: Geist 48px weight 300
  • Padding: 52px all sides
